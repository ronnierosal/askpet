color, high contrast, shounen-manga energy. Kid-friendly (ages 6-12): heroic and
fun, never scary or violent, fully clothed, no blood.

For each character's expression set: SAME character — identical hair, outfit, face
shape and proportions — with ONLY the expression changing. Generate them back-to-
back and tell ChatGPT "same character, new expression, identical design."
