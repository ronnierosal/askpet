KEEPING IT ORIGINAL  (not Beyblade)
-----------------------------------
(I'm not a lawyer — this is just practical guidance.) The general IDEA of spinning-
top battling and ripcord launchers isn't owned by anyone. What IS protected are
specific DESIGNS, character/creature designs, brand names and logos. So to stay
clearly in the clear:
  - Make the TOPS and the LAUNCHERS clearly ORIGINAL — your own silhouette and
    style, NOT a copy of any real toy. Our tops are "spirit-beast blades": each
    has its own animal-spirit motif (below).
  - NO real brand names, logos, or mascots anywhere in the art.
  - When prompting, add this line: "original design, not based on any existing toy,
    game, or brand."
  - Our text is already original (e.g. the launch cry is "Spirits, fly!", not any
    trademarked slogan).

==================================================================
The idea (yours!): a spinner THROWS their top, then weaves their fingers through
quick hand signs to channel and steer their spirit-beast — a cool "spirit-weaving"
move.

IS THIS COPYRIGHT-SAFE? (not legal advice) — yes, with care. Finger hand-signs /
"mudras" are REAL traditional gestures (used in Buddhism and martial arts for
centuries); a gesture itself can't be copyrighted, and "hand signs to focus power"
is a generic idea. What you must NOT copy is another work's specific character
designs, art style, or technique NAMES. So:
  - Use OUR characters and our OWN term ("spirit-weaving" / "blade-binding"),
    never another franchise's jutsu / technique names or logos.
  - Draw original hands and poses; don't trace or imitate a specific show's frames.
  - Add to each prompt: "original spirit-weaving hand sign, not from any existing
    anime, game, or brand."
